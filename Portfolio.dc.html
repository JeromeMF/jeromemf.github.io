03 / About

I like the part of the job where nothing exists yet: no team, no architecture, no release process. I also like the part a few years later where shipping has become routine.

My background is mainly native iOS, with some Flutter along the way, but most of what I do now is people and delivery: hiring, coaching, 1:1s, and translating what the business wants into plans engineers can actually commit to. I've spent a big part of my career in startups and scale-ups, largely in fitness, wellness, and mental health, joining early and staying through the messy growth stage. Earlier on I worked mainly on video streaming and media consumption apps, plus a shorter stretch at SIBS (banking) on high-security transactional systems, which is where I learned to care about the unglamorous parts.
